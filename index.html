Analyst Program

The Analyst Program is designed for underclassmen and new members with no prior finance experience. In addition to the general member meeting, analysts meet weekly for dedicated training sessions to build the skills needed to succeed in UFA and beyond.

Each week, our Directors of Investments and Consulting lead sessions covering accounting, valuation, financial modeling, and market analysis: everything you need to hit the ground running in UFA and in recruiting.

Apply to Analyst Program
01
Explore Both Paths

Gain exposure to high finance and consulting through weekly lectures and assignments that build knowledge from the ground up.

02
Engage and Apply

Participate in stock pitches, case-style exercises, and hands-on projects — turning technical concepts into practical experience.

03
Grow Your Network

Connect with a community of like-minded peers while receiving insights on recruiting, resume formatting, and networking.

Analyst Competitions

Every analyst competes in the fall and the spring.

Fall Semester

Stock Pitch Competition

Analyst teams select any publicly traded stock on a U.S. exchange, build their investment thesis, and defend it in front of a room. Top teams advance to a final round.

Format — Round 1 open to all analyst teams. Top 6 advance to finals.
Universe — Any stock listed on a U.S. exchange. Team selects the company.
Judges — UFA upperclassmen and finance alumni.
Prize pool — $1,000 split across 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.
Spring Semester

Case Competition

Analyst teams are assigned a real corporate case and given approximately one week to develop and present their recommendation.

Format — Round 1 open to all analyst teams. Top 6 advance to finals.
Client — A real corporation. Case details released one week before presentations.
Judges — Active industry professionals from the finance and business community.
Prize pool — $1,000 split across 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.

Investment Team

The Investment Team is where serious finance preparation meets a tight-knit community. You'll build the technical skills recruiters want and the relationships that last long after graduation.

The technical training is rigorous. But ask any Investment Team member what they remember most, and they'll tell you it's the people.

What sets us apart

Start here. Go anywhere.

UFA is built around you. We offer the breadth, access, and community to explore every corner of finance on your own terms, without locking you into a single path before you're ready.

01

We bring the industry to you

From bulge-bracket bankers to PE professionals, we bring real practitioners into the room so you do not have to cold-email your way into a conversation.

02

The widest curriculum on campus

IB, PE, asset management, consulting, corporate finance, tech. UFA is the only finance organization at UT that covers all of it under one roof, so you can explore before you commit.

03

Involvement at every level

No matter where you are in your finance journey, there is a place for you in UFA. General Member, Analyst, or Associate, you choose how deep you go.

04

A community every week

Every Wednesday we feed you. Showing up is easy when there is a meal waiting, and it is part of what makes UFA feel like a community rather than just another club.

75%

A clear path upward

Three in four Investment and Consulting Associates began in the Analyst Program. UFA rewards commitment, and the Analyst Program is the proven pipeline into our most selective teams.
