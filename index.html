About UFA

The oldest McCombs-affiliated organization on campus

Founded in 1951, UFA is the oldest McCombs-affiliated organization at UT Austin. What started as a small group of students passionate about finance has grown into a community of 300+ members with alumni at the world's top firms.

Our mission remains unchanged: to provide a welcoming, high-impact environment where any student — regardless of background or experience — can explore their curiosity about finance and build the skills to act on it.

Entry-level · One round of applications

Analyst Program

The Analyst Program serves as the foundation for your journey in UFA. Designed for underclassmen and new members, the program offers an additional weekly meeting beyond the general member meeting — giving you more opportunities to get involved and meet new people.

Each week, our Directors of Investment and Consulting lead sessions covering the fundamentals — accounting, valuation, financial modeling, and market analysis. It's the foundation that prepares you for everything else UFA has to offer.

Apply to Analyst Program
01
Explore Both Paths

Gain exposure to high-finance and consulting through weekly lectures and assignments that build knowledge from the ground up.

02
Engage and Apply

Participate in stock pitches, case-style exercises, and hands-on projects — turning technical concepts into practical experience.

03
Grow Your Network

Connect with a community of like-minded peers while receiving insights on recruiting, resume formatting, and networking.

Selective · Investment track

Investment Team

The Investment Associate Program is where serious finance preparation meets a tight-knit community. You'll build the technical skills recruiters want — and the relationships that last long after graduation.

The technical training is rigorous. But ask any Investment Associate what they remember most, and they'll tell you it's the people.

Apply to Investment Associates Work with us
Mentorship

Get paired with UFA upperclassmen who mentor you throughout the year — guiding and teaching you what they wished they knew when they were in your shoes.

Market Watch

Dive deep into technicals, track economic trends, and discuss major news weekly. These sessions sharpen your ability to think critically about financial developments.

Stock Pitch

Build and present stock pitches by industry. Research the company in-depth, develop thesis points, model cash flows, analyze risk, and compile research into our published journal.

Program Core Competencies

01

Active Fund Management

Experience real-world portfolio management by analyzing market trends and pitching actionable investment strategies.

02

Modeling Practice

Master Excel-based financial modeling, including DCF analysis, comparable company analysis, and LBO modeling.

03

Equity Research

Contribute to our flagship publication. Analysts perform deep-dives into public companies for our semesterly report.

04

Applied Learning

Bridge the gap between the classroom and the industry with case studies designed to simulate real finance roles.

05

Leadership Roles

Take on responsibility early. Our members lead project teams and manage external firm relations.

06

Recruiting Support

Access specialized resources for technical interview prep and networking strategies for high-finance placements.

Selective · Consulting track

Consulting Team

UFA's Consulting Associates tackle real business problems, build client-ready deliverables, and develop the structured thinking that top firms look for.

Outside the casework, you'll find a group that genuinely enjoys spending time together, from team socials and dinners to friendships that last long after graduation.

Apply to Consulting Associates Work with us
Case Coaching

Practice live case interviews. Sharpen your structured thinking and communication under realistic interview conditions.

Industry Deep Dives

Explore consulting frameworks applied to real industries. Sessions cover market sizing, competitive analysis, and strategic recommendations.

Client Simulations

Build and present consulting deliverables to simulated clients. Develop slide decks, synthesize findings, and defend your recommendations.

Member-authored research

Our Investment Associates publish in-depth equity research and market analysis that reflects the real-world rigor we bring to every assignment.

Each report is student-authored and peer-reviewed within the team — members select companies, build financial models, develop investment theses, and compile their findings into a professional publication.
